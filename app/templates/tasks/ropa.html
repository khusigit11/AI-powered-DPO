What is ROPA?

A Record of Processing Activities is a document that maps out all the personal data your organisation collects and processes. Under GDPR Article 30, organisations are required to maintain this record. It answers the fundamental questions: what data do we hold, why do we hold it, who can see it, and how long do we keep it.

Without knowing what data you hold, you cannot protect it. This is the foundation of everything a DPO does.
