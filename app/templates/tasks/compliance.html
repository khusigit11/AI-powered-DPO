Why this matters: A DPO regularly checks whether the organisation is following data protection rules. This is like a health check for your data practices. Answer each question honestly — the AI will tell you what needs fixing and how to fix it.

Compliance Questions

1. Do you only collect personal data that you actually need?

For example, if you run an online shop, do you only ask for the information needed to process an order — or do you also collect things like date of birth when it's not necessary?

2. Is your legal reason for collecting data written down?

Every type of personal data you collect needs a legal reason — like consent, a contract, or a legal obligation. Is this documented somewhere?

3. Do you get proper consent when it's needed?

When you rely on consent as your legal reason, do people actively agree — like ticking a box — rather than being opted in by default?

4. Is the personal data you hold accurate and up to date?

Do you have a way for people to update their details? Are records checked and corrected regularly?

5. Do you delete data when you no longer need it?

Do you have set time limits for how long you keep different types of data? Do you actually follow them?

6. Do you have security measures protecting personal data?

Things like passwords, encryption, locked cabinets, access controls — anything that stops unauthorised people from seeing the data.

7. Do you have a process for handling data breaches?

If personal data was stolen or leaked tomorrow, does your team know what to do? Is there a written plan?

8. Has someone been given the DPO role?

Is there a specific person responsible for data protection in your organisation — whether full time, part time, or outsourced?
