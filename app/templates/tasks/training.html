Why Staff Training Matters

The biggest cause of data breaches is not sophisticated hacking — it is human error. Someone emails a spreadsheet of customer details to the wrong person. Someone clicks a phishing link. Someone leaves their laptop unlocked on a train. GDPR Article 39(1)(a) says the DPO must raise awareness and train staff who are involved in data processing.

Regular training makes sure everyone in the organisation knows the basics: what counts as personal data, how to handle it properly, how to spot a phishing email, what to do if they suspect a breach, and what they should never do with sensitive information. The DPO runs these sessions, tracks who has completed them, and keeps records as proof for regulators.
